Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how msging ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used for authentication and email verification.
  • Display name — the name you choose to display to other users.

1.2 Messaging Data

When you use the App, we store:

  • Messages — text content you send in direct messages and group chats.
  • Conversation metadata — including participants, group names, and message timestamps.
  • Online status — whether you are currently active or offline.

1.3 Device Information

We do not collect device identifiers, location data, contacts, camera, photos, or any other device-level information.

2. How We Use Your Information

PurposeDetails
AuthenticationEmail and password to verify your identity
Message deliveryStore and deliver messages to intended recipients
User discoveryAllow others to find you by email to start conversations
Online presenceShow other users whether you are online or offline

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any form of automated decision-making.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, located in the asia-south2 region.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest by Google Cloud.
  • Firestore security rules ensure that only authenticated users can access data, and users can only read/write their own profile and messages they are participants in.
  • Messages cannot be edited or deleted after being sent.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only accessible to:

  • You — your own profile and messages.
  • Your conversation partners — messages are visible to participants of each conversation.
  • Google/Firebase — as the infrastructure provider, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

  • Your account and messages are retained until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your profile and conversation entries are removed.
  • Messages in group chats may persist after account deletion as part of the group history.
  • We may retain anonymized or aggregated data for service improvement.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Export your data (contact us via GitHub Issues).
  • Withdraw consent at any time by stopping use of the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

9. Open Source

msging is open-source. You can review the full source code, including how data is handled, at github.com/4XW1N/msging.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
